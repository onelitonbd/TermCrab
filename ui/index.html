How dreaming works
While the agent runs, facts land in short-term daily notes. During a dream sweep those notes are scored: the keepers are promoted into MEMORY.md (long-term), the rest fade, and a short line is written to the dream diary. Nothing is sent anywhere — all of it stays as plain files on this device.
